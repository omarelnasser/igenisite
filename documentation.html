    iGeni Zoom Integration Guide

    This document details how teachers and administrators use and manage the Zoom integration within the iGeni application.

    1. Adding / Connecting the App

    1. Open the iGeni app and navigate to the Timetable tab.
    2. Select a current scheduled session.
    3. Tap the orange Connect Zoom button.
    4. Sign in with your Zoom credentials and click Allow to grant meeting provisioning permissions.
    5. The button in iGeni will automatically turn into Start Zoom Meeting.

    2. Using the App

    • Starting Meetings: Tap Start Zoom Meeting on an active session to provision an instant meeting under your Zoom host account and automatically open the call in the Zoom client.
    • Automated Attendance: As students join and leave via the class link, iGeni tracks their connected time. Once the meeting ends, students meeting the 80% attendance duration threshold are automatically marked present.

    3. Removing / Disconnecting the Integration

    1. Log in to your Zoom Account and navigate to the Zoom App Marketplace.
    2. Click Manage > Added Apps.
    3. Locate iGeni Attendance Taker 2.0 and click Remove.
    4. Confirm removal to immediately revoke all stored OAuth access tokens.
