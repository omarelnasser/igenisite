Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

iGeni ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application and integrated services, including our Zoom integration.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Teacher email addresses and account identification managed via Supabase authentication.
  • Zoom Integration Data: OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, token expiration timestamps, and Zoom profile email address.
  • Session & Attendance Logs: Zoom meeting identifiers, meeting participant usernames, join timestamps, and departure timestamps strictly used for attendance recording.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provision dynamic live class Zoom meetings on behalf of authorized teachers.
  • Automate participant attendance collection based on lecture duration thresholds.
  • Sync scheduled sessions with classroom calendars.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Zoom OAuth data to third parties. Ephemeral timeline log workspace data collected during live sessions is automatically purged after attendance processing completes.

4. Data Subject Rights & Removal

Users have full control over their integrated accounts. You may revoke iGeni's access to your Zoom account at any time by navigating to your Zoom App Marketplace Installed Apps page and removing the iGeni integration. To request full deletion of your user data, contact support.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@igeni.app.
